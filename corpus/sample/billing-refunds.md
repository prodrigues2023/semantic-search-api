A refund can be issued within 90 days of the original charge. Refunds go back to the original payment method and typically post within five to ten business days depending on the card issuer.

Partial refunds are supported: specify the amount when issuing the refund, and the remainder stays charged to the customer. A full refund automatically cancels any pending subscription renewal tied to that charge.
