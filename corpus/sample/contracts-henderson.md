The Henderson contract renews automatically each January unless either party gives ninety days' written notice. The current term includes a volume discount that steps down if Henderson's annual spend falls below the threshold set in Schedule B.

Any change to the Henderson contract's payment terms requires sign-off from both the account owner and legal, since the original agreement was negotiated directly with Henderson's general counsel.
